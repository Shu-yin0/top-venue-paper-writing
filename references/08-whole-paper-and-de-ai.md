# Whole paper and de-AI pass

- Write the one-sentence causal chain, then check each section's hook is the next link. Abstract, intro and conclusion state the mechanism in the same shape.
- High-risk words pass an evidence gate (see 01). Every claim carries its protocol adverbial. Negative results appear once in the body at the place they bear, with at most a pointer elsewhere. Terms keep one definition throughout.
- Packaging tools, use at least three: a name for the idea, a dual-tension phrase, failure-first ordering, a closed loop back to the opening, a small library of sentence patterns.
- grep list (zero hits or a reason to keep each): delve, underscore, multifaceted, pivotal, leverage, foster, nuanced, poised, unveil, showcasing, commendable, intricate, realm, meticulous, tapestry, revolutionize, holistic, moreover, furthermore, "it is worth noting", "in recent years", "attracted increasing attention", "paves the way", remarkably, significantly (without a test), novel, harness, enables (without a metric).
- Structure-level fingerprints to check by reading aloud: equal-length sentence rhythm, triple parallelism, First/Second/Third chains, empty transition sentences, uniform hedging.
- Deletion test: delete each sentence; if nothing is lost it was idle. Also hand the intro and conclusion to someone who did not take part and ask only "where is it boring, where do you not believe it".
- Scan your own new text for self-weakening words before handing it to the authors.

## Patterns and templates
- Whole-paper score: one root-cause chain organises all parts (not parallel selling points); the seven parts are seven cuts of one chain. Hooks by part: abstract = the phenomenon or contrast sentence; intro = P1 ends on an observable failure, P2 a question or dual tension; related work = the first sentence of each neighbour already contrasts; method = a causal first sentence per subsection; experiments = a question per subsection; limitations = a positive design-logic opening; conclusion = the recall sentence.
- Five packaging tools: a name (a mechanism compressed into a phrase), dual tension (each of two families has one causal defect), failure-first (concrete, observable, attributed to a mechanism), the closed loop (every claim paired with "if the mechanism is true we should observe X", ablation = the mechanism broken), a sentence library plus the six number forms. Packaging acts on structure, not adjectives.
- High-risk words with an evidence gate: unified, world model, physical reasoning, SOTA chains, remarkably, zero-shot, robust, enables. Replace by a scoped statement or the number.
- Two layers of AI fingerprints: vocabulary (the grep list) and structure (equal-length rhythm, triple parallelism, Moreover/Furthermore chains, idle transitions such as "It is worth noting that", uniform hedging, template openings such as "In recent years ... has attracted increasing attention"). The opposite of AI-sounding is specificity: concrete hyper-parameters, concrete failure scenes, trade-off sentences ("we considered X but rejected it because Y"), uneven rhythm with short sentences for the key point. Keep the functional slot of a template, never its sentence.
- Per-section de-AI prescriptions: abstract = drop "In recent years" openers and swap adjectives for numbers or protocol adverbials; intro = each paragraph's first sentence is a concrete phenomenon, never "plays a pivotal role"; related work = mechanism-level contrast, not "while impressive, it suffers from"; method = Because-fails sentences, trade-off sentences and concrete hyper-parameters, "use" instead of leverage/harness; experiments = delete remarkably/significantly without statistics; limitations = a concrete phenomenon with "likely"; conclusion = past tense, no "In conclusion" or "paves the way".
- Three final checks: word-frequency grep, reading aloud, sentence deletion test. Finish with the final checks in 09 before submitting.
