# Cold-read brief

Give the reader: title, abstract, Figure 1 (with caption), the first two intro paragraphs, the main results table, and the conclusion. Nothing else, no explanation.

Ask exactly these, in writing, before any discussion:
1. In one sentence: what is the claim?
2. What number would you quote to a colleague, and under which protocol?
3. Which sentence did you not believe, and why?
4. Where were you bored or lost?
5. What is the nearest prior work you expected to see compared, and was it?

Record answers verbatim; do not defend the draft during the read.
